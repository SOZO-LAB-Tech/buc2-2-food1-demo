Consumer evidence demo · Nestlé Vietnam

From what consumers say to what to build next

Two connected prototypes. The Consumer Truth Hub gathers feedback from every channel and maps it to the jobs consumers are trying to get done. The Outcome Survey asks parents to rate those needs, so the team can see which ones matter most and are served worst.

For Nestlé teams · Web app

Consumer Truth Hub

One place to read consumer feedback across 18 brands, tie it to customer jobs and outcomes, and see where needs are underserved.

  • Inbox and mapping for feedback from Shopee, Lazada, Careline, Facebook and complaints
  • Survey results and an Innovation Landscape of underserved needs
  • Chat with Evidence: ask a question, get an answer with cited sources

For parents · Mobile survey

Outcome Survey · Kids’ energy drinks

What parents receive by Zalo or email. They rate the needs behind giving their child Milo for school and sport.

  • Importance first, then satisfaction, on a 5-point scale
  • One statement per screen, with keyboard shortcuts on desktop
  • Ends by showing the parent the need with their biggest gap

How the two connect

The Hub finds the needs; the survey measures them. Each step opens the matching screen.

  1. 1Listen

    Feedback from Shopee, Lazada, Careline, Facebook and the complaints system lands in one Inbox.

    Hub › Inbox
  2. 2Map

    Each piece of feedback is tied to a step of the customer’s job and the outcome they want.

    Hub › Mapping
  3. 3Ask

    Parents rate those outcomes: how important each one is, and how satisfied they are today.

    Outcome Survey
  4. 4Prioritise

    Results show which needs matter a lot but are poorly served, ready for the innovation team.

    Hub › Surveys

About this demo

  • Sample data onlyBrand names are real; every figure, person and comment is made up for the demo.
  • Nothing is saved or sentEverything stays in your browser tab, and reloading the Hub resets it. The survey’s auto-save, “Finish later” link and top-up are simulated.
  • Chat with Evidence answers offline hereIt answers from the evidence built into the page. Hosted inside Claude, it can answer free-form with Claude.
